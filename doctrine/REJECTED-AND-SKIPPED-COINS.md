# Your rejected / checked-not-added ledger

**This file is yours and it starts empty.** It is where YOU record coins you looked at and
decided against, so you do not spend the same hour twice.

It ships blank on purpose. An earlier version of this product shipped the author's own list of
74 rejections and made checking it mandatory before any discovery run, which meant every
member's results were silently pruned by somebody else's taste, on reasons like "ugly chart" and
"I don't want it" — with no way to know it had happened. That was the same defect as a hardcoded
skip list, and it is fixed the same way: your ledger, your calls.

For worked examples of what a good rejection looks like, see `examples/EXAMPLE-rejections.md`.
Those are illustrations and they filter nothing.

## How to use it

After each research session, append a row for anything you examined and passed on. Date it and
give the real reason, in enough detail that future-you can tell whether the reason still holds.

A rejection is not permanent. Prices move, teams ship, narratives arrive. Re-check anything here
whenever the reason you wrote down might have stopped being true, and say so when you do.
