## THE THESIS, IN HIS TERMS

1. **No single chain wins everything.** There will not be one chain that does everything for everyone.
2. **Each chain will have a specific purpose.** His working examples: Ethereum for security, Solana for
   speed of transaction.
3. **He is explicitly uncertain about the outcome** ("To be honest, I don't know what to expect"), so
   the framework is a tool for thinking, not a prediction he is committed to. Do not overstate his
   conviction back to him.

## THE FRAMEWORK, AS HE STATED IT

Four steps, at the CHAIN level:

1. **Name the chain's unique proposition.** What is it actually best at, not what it markets.
2. **Map it to the real world.** What real-world function does that unique proposition replace, or do
   better than the incumbent?
3. **Size that real-world segment's revenue.** Actual dollars the incumbent industry earns.
4. **Multiply by the token's value-accrual percentage**, based on the token's actual mechanism.

This is the CHAIN-LEVEL version of the 8-step comps chain already in
`Hedge-Fund-DB/HEDGE-FUND-DB-SPEC.md`. The important difference and the reason to keep this separate:
**the siphon must be tied to the chain's SPECIFIC EDGE, not to the whole addressable market.** A chain
whose edge is speed gets credit against the segments where speed is the binding constraint, and no
credit anywhere else.

## THE MISSING TERM I FLAGGED BACK TO HIM (2026-07-30)

As stated, the framework can only produce a positive number, because every input is positive. For
inflationary chains it needs a **fifth step: subtract issuance.** SOL is the proof: fee value reaching
holders is on the order of tens of millions per year against roughly $1.74B a year of new supply
printed. Skipping the dilution term makes an unambiguously negative asset look mildly positive.

**Recommended full chain:**
unique proposition → real-world segment it replaces → segment revenue → siphon % (tied to the specific
edge) → token capture % (mechanism-verified) → **minus annual issuance** → apply multiple → implied
market cap → divide by circulating supply → target price.

## WHY THIS FRAMEWORK IS VALUABLE EVEN WHEN IT RETURNS NEAR ZERO

Applied honestly to SOL and XLM on 2026-07-30, it returned "the token captures almost nothing" in both
cases. That is not a failure of the framework, it is the framework working. **It forces you to name the
real reason you would own the asset** (monetary premium, mechanical reserve demand, or plain market
beta) instead of hiding behind a fee story that the arithmetic does not support.
