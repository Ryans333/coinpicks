# <span color="yellow_bg">**Overview**</span><span color="yellow_bg">:</span>
**Aerodrome ($AERO)** is the main place where people swap tokens on **Base** — Coinbase's own blockchain. A "DEX" (decentralized exchange) is basically a self-service version of Coinbase: instead of Coinbase manually approving every coin you can trade, a DEX lets you swap any token directly from your wallet. Aerodrome is the **largest DEX on Base by far** — it holds more than **half of all Base DEX trading volume** — and it's been publicly endorsed by the Coinbase / Base core team. The **$AERO token** pays liquidity providers, controls governance, and shares in protocol revenue (the platform is pulling in approximately **$100M annualized in fees and revenue** as of early 2026). **Big news as of Q1–Q2 2026:** Aerodrome and its sister project Velodrome are **merging into a unified protocol called "Aero"**, which is set to launch on Ethereum mainnet and Circle's new "Arc" chain in Q2 2026 — pushing the protocol well beyond its Base-only roots.

**What $AERO does:** AERO pays liquidity providers across all the trading pools on Aerodrome. Holders can lock their AERO into veAERO (vote-escrowed AERO) to vote weekly on which pools get the next round of AERO emissions, earning 100% of trading fees and bribes from the pools they vote for — effectively a yield-share for active participants.

**Analogy:**
*Think of Aerodrome as the main marketplace in the center of "Base City", with Coinbase as the city's mayor publicly recommending it to all visitors.* Coinbase the exchange only lists carefully-vetted "safe" coins. But most people also want access to the thousands of newer, smaller, riskier tokens. Instead of putting those directly on Coinbase, Coinbase funnels users into Aerodrome — the official marketplace on its own chain — so they can still find everything else without Coinbase being the one curating it. $AERO is the local currency of that marketplace, used for governance, voting, and getting a cut of the fees the marketplace generates.
