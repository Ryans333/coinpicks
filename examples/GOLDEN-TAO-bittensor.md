# <span color="yellow_bg">**Overview**</span><span color="yellow_bg">:</span>
**("the ai version of bitcoin")** **Bittensor ($TAO)** takes Bitcoin's mining idea and makes it programmable. Bitcoin pays miners for solving math puzzles that only secure the network. Bittensor pays miners for producing useful AI work instead. The team calls it Proof of Useful Work: rather than mining for hashes, you mine for intelligence. Anyone in the world can run an AI model and submit answers, other independent operators ("validators") score whose answers are best, and the highest scorers earn **$TAO**. TAO copies Bitcoin's money design on purpose: a hard cap of **21 million coins**, with **about 11 million mined so far** and **halving events** that cut new issuance in half (the first was December 2025). In Bitcoin terms, it's roughly 2013. The network is split into **128 subnets**, which work like neighborhoods. Each subnet runs its own competition to mine a different AI product (language models, image generation, financial prediction, text-to-speech, and more), and each subnet has its own token with its own 21 million cap. To get a subnet's token you either mine it or stake TAO to obtain it, so there is a direct economic connection between TAO and every subnet, something Ethereum and Solana do not have between their gas coin and the tokens built on top of them. Because of that link, holding TAO is a bit like holding an S&P 500 of all the subnets, while picking the right single subnet can do far better. Individual subnet token market caps currently run from roughly **$1M to $100M** each, and combined they are worth around **$1.5B**.

**What $TAO does:** AI workers ("miners") and judges ("validators") earn TAO for producing and scoring high-quality AI outputs across the network's 128+ subnets, and anyone querying an AI model on Bittensor pays in TAO. To get into a specific subnet, you stake TAO to receive that subnet's own token; staking also earns yield and steers where new emissions flow. So as more people use and bet on subnets, more TAO has to be bought and locked up to take part.

**Analogy:**
*Bittensor is like a city full of science fairs.* Each science fair (a "subnet") focuses on a different topic — one for image-recognition AI, one for text-generation AI, one for financial-prediction AI, etc. Anyone in the world can set up a booth at any fair (a "miner" — meaning someone running an AI model). Judges (called "validators") walk around scoring whose booth is best, and the more skin-in-the-game (TAO staked) each judge has, the more weight their scoring carries. Whoever runs the best booths each day gets paid in TAO. Anyone in the world can then walk up to any booth and pay in TAO to use that AI model.

# <span color="yellow_bg">**Exclusivity Factor:**</span>
- **Ease of Use:**
	The underlying TAO chain is the back-end infrastructure — actual everyday use depends on whichever subnet you want to interact with, since each subnet builds its own website and tools. Navigating the TAO ecosystem itself isn't too complicated, but it does involve a few extra steps like creating a separate wallet (the supported one is called Talisman) and sending funds over to it. Site to explore live subnets: [tao.app/explorer](http://tao.app/explorer).
- **Hair-on-Fire:**
	There's exploding demand for **open-source, cheaper, decentralized alternatives to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google's AI APIs**. Bittensor is the most well-funded and widely-recognized version of this idea in crypto, and it has actual working subnets shipping models and being used in production. **TaoWeave** (a publicly-traded TAO treasury company, similar in shape to MicroStrategy's BTC strategy) reported **$1.7M in Q1 2026 net income driven by $2.2M in unrealized gains on TAO holdings** — meaning institutional capital is flowing in via public-market vehicles.
- **Exclusivity Factor:**
	Bittensor is **the largest decentralized AI network in crypto by liquidity, market cap, and public visibility** — and was the first project to ship the "decentralized AI marketplace" concept at scale. The **"Dynamic TAO" (dTAO) upgrade, live since February 13, 2025**, restructured the whole economy: every subnet now issues its own token (an "alpha" token, each capped at 21M like TAO), and each subnet runs an on-chain automated market-maker pool that holds two reserves: TAO on one side and that subnet's own token on the other. To get a subnet's token you swap TAO into its pool, so your TAO joins the pool's TAO reserve and you receive the subnet token; the token's price is simply how much TAO sits in the pool divided by how many tokens are in it. To exit, you swap back to TAO at whatever the pool price is then, so you can get back more or fewer TAO than you put in. Because the only ways to get a subnet's token are to mine it or to stake TAO, real TAO gets locked into these pools as subnets grow, and there is a direct economic link between TAO and every subnet that Ethereum's and Solana's gas tokens do NOT have with the tokens built on top of them. Daily emissions are routed toward whichever subnets are pulling in the most staked TAO. The subnet ecosystem grew from ~32 active subnets in early 2025 to **128+ by early 2026**, with combined subnet token valuations now around **$1.5B**. Multiple competitors exist (Ritual, Akash, [io.net](http://io.net)) but none have TAO's combination of scale, listings, and treasury-company backing.
