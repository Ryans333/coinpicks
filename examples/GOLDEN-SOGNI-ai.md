# <span color="yellow_bg">**Overview**</span><span color="yellow_bg">:</span>
**Sogni AI ($SOGNI)** is a creative-AI network on **Base** that lets anyone make AI images and video cheaply by renting computing power from a global pool of everyday people's machines (GPUs and Macs) instead of from a big company's data center. It pairs two of crypto's hottest themes: **AI and DePIN** (decentralized physical infrastructure, meaning regular people plug their own hardware into a network and get paid for it). The plan is a creative-AI **"super app"**: a hub of tools (an image/video studio, a mobile app, chat bots, games, NFT tools, an artist registry, and storefronts that sell physical prints) all running on its own decentralized GPU network. It was founded by former **CoinMarketCap** leaders, ran roughly **9 months of testnet** on Base, and reports about **350,000 lifetime users** (Season 2 alone: ~84,000 users, 498 GPUs, and 134 million creations).
The simplest way to place it against the project everyone compares it to: **Render is the "AWS for GPU rendering"** (the raw infrastructure layer), while **Sogni is the "Adobe Creative Cloud + App Store" sitting on top** — the consumer-facing application layer and marketplace, built on Base.
**What $SOGNI does:** You spend SOGNI to generate images and content, and the GPU/Mac operators who run those jobs earn SOGNI, so it's the settlement currency between users and node operators. Honest note on value: demand is **usage-driven** (more generations means more SOGNI needed to pay for compute), but there is **no confirmed buyback or burn** — so value only accrues if real, paid usage of the network keeps growing, not through any automatic mechanism that shrinks supply.
**Analogy:**
*It's a cheaper, co-owned version of Adobe or Google Veo.* Instead of paying one big company for AI image/video tools, you tap a worldwide pool of strangers' computers, and the people lending those computers get paid in the token.

# <span color="yellow_bg">**Exclusivity Factor:**</span>
- **Ease of Use:**
	Connect a wallet (or just buy in-house "credits") and start generating. In a hands-on test, Sogni came out to roughly **$0.025 per image versus Adobe Firefly's ~$0.08**, and where Firefly caps you at 2,500 images a month, Sogni is unlimited. Image generation is solid; video generation is still early and lower quality.
- **Hair-on-Fire:**
	The best creative AI (OpenAI's Sora, Google's Veo) is locked up by a few giants and can cost **$2,400+ per year**, pricing out normal people. Digital content creation is a **~$32–33B market**, and global ad spend topped **$1 trillion in 2025** (over 73% digital). Sogni's pitch is putting cheap, unlimited creative AI in everyone's hands.
- **Exclusivity Factor:**
	It's an early mover on a **consumer-facing decentralized creative-AI "super app"** (Adobe Creative Cloud + app store, on Base), run by an unusually well-connected team (ex-CoinMarketCap leadership, ex-Disney/Adobe creative lead). Beyond the team and the Render-style lineage, the moat is thin: the underlying AI models are open, and competitors (Grok Imagine, Sora, Veo) are far better funded.

# <span color="yellow_bg">Narrative Scoring:</span>
### **Narrative Maturity Score**
Stage 5 — the DePIN-compute field is crowded. Sogni's twist is fusing AI with graphics for everyday consumers. The narrative is mature, but the token has never captured a spotlight, and the chart is down ~90% from its August 2025 high.
### **Smart Money Compatibility Score**
The real draw is the team's pedigree (ex-CoinMarketCap CTO/CIO, a Disney/Adobe creative lead) and its advisors (the operator who scaled Udemy for Business to $100M ARR, and a former CEO of Kraken US). On-market signals are weak, though: a small main DEX pool (~$240K), and MEXC/Kraken/Gate listings with thin volume.
### **Narrative Communication Score**
Decent brand with shout-outs from CoinMarketCap, Kraken, and Gate, and the broader creative-AI narrative is loud right now (Elon's Grok Imagine, OpenAI's Sora 2 vs Google's Veo 3). But Sogni itself hasn't broken into the spotlight, and the price action shows no momentum.
### **Narrative Lineage Score**
A direct descendant of Render's 2021 DePIN-compute run (a proven ~100x trend) crossed with the broader AI wave — bringing creative AI to "the people" instead of the giants.
### **Narrative Mutation Score**
The mutation: pointing decentralized compute at consumer creative content (images and video) and bundling it into a super-app plus marketplace, rather than renting raw GPU power to professionals.
