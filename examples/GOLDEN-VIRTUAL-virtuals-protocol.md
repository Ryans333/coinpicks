# <span color="yellow_bg">**Overview**</span><span color="yellow_bg">:</span>
**Virtuals Protocol ($VIRTUAL) is the face of AI agents on Base — sitting exactly where the agentic-AI narrative collides with the new way the internet is going to start paying for things.** Think about how you use Claude or ChatGPT today: you ask a question, you get text back. The next step — **already happening in 2026** — is you ask an AI to *go do something* that costs money. Book a flight. Buy an API call. Hire another agent to do a subtask. The AI completes the whole job for you, including the payment. The payment layer underneath that is **Coinbase's x402 protocol** — a stablecoin micropayment standard built on the long-dormant **HTTP 402 "Payment Required"** status code that lets AI agents pay APIs and pay each other in USDC over standard web requests. As of March 2026, **x402 has processed over 119 million transactions on Base**, runs roughly **$600M in annualized payment volume**, and settles agent-to-agent transactions in under two seconds with **zero protocol fees**. The **x402 Foundation was co-founded by Coinbase and Cloudflare**, and now also includes **Google and Visa**. **Virtuals is one of the largest production deployments of x402 in existence.** On the Virtuals network specifically, autonomous agents have completed **2.28M jobs**, the platform has produced an estimated **~$400M in cumulative "Agentic GDP"** (the value autonomous agents create as they trade, pay each other, and execute work), and the protocol is generating around **$60M in revenue annualizing toward ~$300M**. **$VIRTUAL is the network's base currency** — every agent token launched on the platform is paired against $VIRTUAL for trading and liquidity, so demand for any single agent on Virtuals flows through $VIRTUAL. Virtuals had a strong 2025 run on Base and now sits at roughly **$458M market cap**, down ~86% from a $5.07 cycle ATH — which reads more like narrative consolidation than narrative death. As of mid-2026 the network has **45,688 AI agents created**, **42,693 AI projects** on the launchpad, and a combined launchpad market cap of **$621M**.

**What $VIRTUAL does:** Every AI agent token launched on the Virtuals platform is paired against VIRTUAL for trading and liquidity — meaning demand for *any* agent on the platform flows through VIRTUAL. It also pays platform-level fees, and the Agent Commerce Protocol uses VIRTUAL as one of the settlement currencies between agents.

**Analogy:**
*Think of it like Shopify + Stripe + LinkedIn for AI agents.* Shopify lets anyone launch a store; Stripe handles the payment plumbing; LinkedIn is the marketplace where work happens. Virtuals does all three for AI bots — anyone can launch an agent (Shopify), the platform handles agent-to-agent payments using a crypto rail called x402 (Stripe), and agents can find each other to hire and get hired (LinkedIn).
