# <span color="yellow_bg">**Overview**</span><span color="yellow_bg">:</span>
**Zama ($ZAMA)** is a privacy add-on for blockchains. Right now, every transaction you do on Ethereum is fully public — your wallet, your balances, your trades, who you sent money to, all visible to anyone who looks. Zama uses a kind of math called **Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)** that lets the blockchain process your data while it stays locked in a sealed box the whole time. Nobody — not the network, not validators, not other users — sees what's inside, but the math still works. Founded in 2020 by **Pascal Paillier** (the mathematician who invented part of this kind of encryption back in 1999) and **Rand Hindi** (who previously built and sold a privacy startup to Sonos). Crossed $1B valuation in June 2025 and launched on Ethereum December 30, 2025.

**What $ZAMA does:** It's the protocol's gas token — every confidential transaction on Zama's network pays fees in ZAMA, and those fees get permanently destroyed (burned), so the more people use the network, the smaller the total ZAMA supply gets.

**Analogy:**
*Think postcard vs. sealed envelope.* Every blockchain transaction today is a postcard — anyone watching can read what's on it. Zama turns every transaction into a sealed envelope. The network can still deliver it and even do math on what's inside (add up values, check rules, run logic), but no one ever opens the envelope.

# <span color="yellow_bg">**Exclusivity Factor:**</span>
- **Ease of Use:**
  Developers don't need to know any of the heavy math. They write normal smart contract code (Solidity, the standard language) and the privacy layer works underneath — kind of like how website developers don't need to understand HTTPS for their site to be secure, it just works. JPMorgan's blockchain division (called Kinexys, formerly Onyx) used Zama in a real-world project called "Project EPIC," meaning a top-3 global bank actually built with this tech, not just talked about it.
- **Hair-on-Fire:**
  Public blockchains have a huge privacy problem. Every wallet, every balance, every trade is visible to anyone. This is one of the biggest reasons banks and big companies have stayed off public blockchains — they literally can't operate in public. It's also why automated bots can spy on pending trades and jump ahead of regular users. Within weeks of launch, more than **$121M of value** was already running through encrypted Zama transactions. JPMorgan Kinexys and **T-REX** (a tokenization platform planning to put **$100B of real-world assets** on chain) both signed on as partners — concrete signals that big institutional players need this kind of privacy to actually use blockchains.
- **Exclusivity Factor:**
  Zama is the **first privacy-focused startup to hit "unicorn" status (over $1B valuation)** in this corner of crypto, and the **largest dedicated FHE research team in the world** (204 employees as of January 2026, ~40% with PhDs, 26 nationalities). Pascal Paillier won the **IACR Fellowship in 2025** — basically the lifetime achievement award in cryptography, the field's equivalent of a Nobel. They raised **$130M total** from Multicoin Capital, Protocol Labs, Pantera Capital, and Blockchange Ventures. Beyond the firms: the founders of three of the biggest blockchains in the industry — **Anatoly Yakovenko (Solana), Gavin Wood (Polkadot + co-founder of Ethereum), and Juan Benet (Filecoin)** — all wrote personal checks. Three rival L1 founders backing the same privacy layer is unusual.

### **Narrative Maturity Score**

### **Smart Money Compatibility Score**

Series B ($57M) co-led by **Pantera Capital and Blockchange Ventures** at $1B+ valuation. Personal checks from **Anatoly Yakovenko (Solana), Gavin Wood (Polkadot/ETH founder), and Juan Benet (Filecoin)** — the founders of three of the biggest L1s in the industry. JPMorgan's Kinexys division partnered for Project EPIC. T-REX selected Zama as default FHE layer for tokenizing $100B in RWAs.

### **Narrative Communication Score**
Rand Hindi does a lot of media interviews and is comfortable explaining FHE in plain language to non-technical audiences. Zama ran their own token launch as a sealed-bid auction using their own privacy tech — the auction was oversubscribed by 218% and cleared around $0.05. That counts as the product being used live, on itself.

### **Narrative Lineage Score**
Privacy on blockchains has a long history. Monero and Zcash are the predecessors. ZK-rollups added some privacy features but mostly for scaling. FHE is a different approach that lets the network compute on data without ever seeing it. Confidential real-world assets, encrypted payroll, private DeFi, and AI processing on private data all need something like this to exist on public chains.

### **Narrative Mutation Score**
Past privacy projects were standalone chains (Monero, Zcash) — you had to leave Ethereum and move to a different chain to get privacy. Zama's approach is the opposite: bolt onto existing chains as an add-on. Structurally similar to how Layer 2s (Arbitrum, Optimism) added scaling on top of Ethereum without replacing it.
